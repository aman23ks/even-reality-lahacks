# Compassion Coach (Compassion Coach): demo video plan

**Length:** about 2 minutes (judges skim; under 2:30 is ideal)
**Format:** split screen. Left: you wearing G2 (phone or webcam). Right: **through-the-lens mirror** (`/mirror.html`). End with the phone dashboard.
**Required by Even:** *show the G2 experience* (the lens mirror counts as through-the-lens footage) and say the **Even Hub app name: Compassion Coach**.

---

## Setup (15 minutes)

1. **Run the app:** `cd "/Users/aman/Desktop/Even Reality" && npm run start:demo`, then scan the QR in **Even Hub → Scan QR**. (Or use the Beta from the glasses menu.)
2. **Lens mirror on the laptop:** open `http://localhost:5173/mirror.html` full screen. Every lens frame and a caption of what was said appear here.
3. **Clean start:** on the phone, **Settings → Reset all data**, hatch Mochi ("Hi Mochi"), say **"Mochi, learn my voice"** and read the 3 lines.
4. **Recording:**
   - Laptop screen: **Cmd+Shift+5 → Record selected portion**, around the mirror.
   - You: a second phone or webcam on a tripod, filming you and your teammate at a desk.
   - Phone screen (for the dashboard shot): iPhone **Control Center → Screen Recording**.
5. **Quiet room:** speech is transcribed live, so background noise hurts.
6. **Teammate:** one person plays the "stressed coworker".

**Tip:** for a fast, predictable take, say **"start the tour"**. Judge mode makes one stressed sentence enough to trigger the nudge, and Mochi reaches Zenith in 3 bounce-backs.

## Editing (iMovie / CapCut / DaVinci, ~20 minutes)

1. Put the **mirror recording** and the **camera footage** side by side (or the mirror full screen with the camera as a small picture-in-picture).
2. Trim the waits. Speech takes about 1.5s to process; cut those pauses.
3. Add short **captions** for the lens text if it's small on screen.
4. Add the title card at the end: *"Compassion Coach · on Even Hub"*.
5. Export at 1080p and upload to **YouTube as Unlisted**. Paste the link into Devpost's **Video demo link**.

## If a take goes wrong
- **No nudge?** Say one more clearly stressed sentence, or type it on the laptop (`http://localhost:5173` → *Try it by typing*; lines starting with `them:` play the teammate) while the mirror records.
- **The reset rejected your answer?** That's fine to keep. It shows the AI actually checks.
- **Need a perfect lens-only take?** Run everything typed in the browser and record just the mirror; the lens visuals are identical to the glasses.
